COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you get started.

A few of the questions people ask most often about the Nuvora experience.

What is Nuvora?

Nuvora is a preventive-health experience designed to help people understand health signals, receive personalized recommendations, and continue tracking progress over time.

What does Nuvora test?

The Nuvora experience can include a broad range of health markers depending on the testing option selected and the member's needs.

Will I speak with a health professional?

Nuvora is designed to include access to expert clinical support as part of the broader experience.

Can I use Nuvora if I already feel healthy?

Preventive health is about understanding your baseline and tracking meaningful changes, not only responding when something feels wrong.

How do I get started?

Begin with the Get Started page and tell us what you want to understand or improve. We'll guide you toward the appropriate next step.
